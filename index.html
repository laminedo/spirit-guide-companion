How your guardian angel reaches you

Guardian angels are the protectors on your spiritual team. They stay close for your whole life and draw nearest when you feel frightened, unsure, vulnerable or in need of reassurance.

Five ways they get through

    How their presence feels

    Steady. Loving. Reassuring.

    That feeling is how you tell them apart. The guide describes true inner guidance as undramatic. It has no need to frighten you or push you.

    Which of these do you already know?

    Repeating numbers

    When a number keeps appearing

    Repetition is one of the easiest ways guidance gets your attention. You see 11:11 on the clock, then 333 on a receipt, then 333 again somewhere unrelated.

    Don't hunt for numbers or read a message into every one. Get curious when the same number keeps finding you.

    111

    A beginning

    Ask yourself

    Treat the number as a nudge to look up. The meaning is in what you were thinking about when it appeared.

    Four steps

    When a sign appears

    A number, a dream, a coincidence, a sudden idea, a nudge. Before you decide what it means, walk through these steps.

    Pause and take a breath

    You don't have to do or decide anything yet. The pause itself is the practice.

    Look at the moment just before

    What were you thinking about? What were you feeling? Were you worried, weighing a decision, asking for help, or thinking of someone? What surrounds a sign often matters as much as the sign.

    Hand on your heart, then ask

    Say this out loud

    I'm here and I'm listening. What do you want me to notice? What would you like me to do with it?

    Don't strain for an answer. Notice what comes on its own. It may be a thought, a feeling, an image, a memory, an idea, or simply knowing. Give the most weight to whatever arrives first, before your mind starts editing.

    Ask for one next step

    No grand plan is required. Ask for a single, simple thing you can do next.

    Seven-day practice

    The listening log

    For seven days, don't go looking for signs. Just pay closer attention. When something catches your eye on its own, write one line about it.

    One line a day is enough. Your first impression, before you tidy it up, is usually the truest.

    0 of 7 days noted

    At the end of the week

    Read your notes back, then answer one question.

    What a week of notes shows you

    • Each kind of guidance has its own feel.
    • Certain signs turn up in certain situations.
    • What you tried to make happen feels different from what simply arrived.

    This is how you build your own language with your guides. Signs only get your attention. Your intuition tells you what they mean, so you never need to depend on them.

    This clears all seven days and your end-of-week answer. It can't be undone.
